## HOW IT WORKS — SIMPLE 3-STEP FLOW

```
Student enters/exits hostel gate
        ↓
Android device with camera recognizes face (< 1 second)
        ↓
Attendance marked instantly + Parent notified on their phone
```

**For Leave (Plan B & C):**
```
Student submits leave request on app (date, reason, type)
        ↓
Parent approves via face verification on their phone
        ↓
Admin/Warden gets final approval — one tap to approve/reject
        ↓
Leave recorded with full audit trail
```
