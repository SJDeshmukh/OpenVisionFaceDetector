## The Problem We Solve

In a typical engineering college:

| Pain Point | Reality |
|---|---|
| Manual roll-call per lecture | 5–10 minutes wasted, 6 lectures/day = **~1 hour lost daily** |
| Proxy attendance fraud | Student marks friend present — undetectable manually |
| Attendance data for NAAC/NBA | Collected in registers, impossible to aggregate quickly |

| 75% attendance rule (UGC/AICTE) | College struggles to identify defaulters early |

**Our system eliminates all of the above.**

---

## How It Works — 3-Step Process

```
Step 1 — Teacher opens web dashboard, selects class + subject
        ↓
Step 2 — Takes ONE photo of the classroom (or uploads from phone)
        ↓
Step 3 — AI detects all faces, matches to enrolled students, marks attendance — DONE in < 30 seconds
```

> No app needed for students. No biometric device needed. Just one photo.
