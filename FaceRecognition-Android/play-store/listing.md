## Full description

TapInX helps authorized organizations manage attendance and daily operations
from one secure mobile app.

Use on-device face matching to record check-in and check-out, continue working
when connectivity is unavailable, and synchronize authorized attendance records
when the device reconnects. Depending on the features enabled by your
organization, TapInX can also support employee or student registration, leave
requests, attendance history, notifications, payroll workflows and hostel
operations.

Key capabilities include:

- Fast on-device face recognition for attendance
- Manual check-in and check-out modes for managed kiosks
- Offline attendance capture with later synchronization
- Configurable organization and tenant controls
- Attendance history and operational reports
- Leave, parent, payroll and hostel workflows when enabled
- Secure production API communication and managed notifications

TapInX is intended for organizations that have provisioned an account. Available
features and data access depend on the administrator's configuration and the
signed-in user's role.
